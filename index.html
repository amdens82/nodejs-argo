The Three Pillars of Sustainability

📉

Reduce

Cut down on the amount of waste we produce. Buy items with less packaging, save water, and switch off unnecessary utilities.

🔄

Reuse

Think twice before throwing things away. Reuse bags, containers, and repair old items instead of buying new ones.

♻️

Recycle

Ensure that paper, plastic, glass, and metal are separated and sent to recycling facilities to be remade into useful products.
